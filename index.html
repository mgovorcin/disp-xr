    disp-xr¶

    Xarray tools for OPERA surface displacement. Open a frame of DISP-S1 or DISP-NISAR granules as one lazy dataset, chain the ministacks, check quality, estimate velocities with honest uncertainties, compare with GNSS, downsample and export, from a laptop to an AWS Batch cluster.

    Get started Tutorials API reference

    import disp_xr as dxr

ds = dxr.open_disp("F11116/")                      # lazy (time, y, x), CRS attached
ds = dxr.chain(dxr.mask(ds, "recommended"))   # displacement from the first date
ds = dxr.rereference(ds, "median")
velocity = dxr.fit_velocity(ds, method="midas")    # robust, with a sigma per pixel

    What you can do¶

    • Open and chain frames

      Local folders, S3 or HTTPS; ministacks chained across reference dates and across product changes; virtual frames that open in seconds.

      Getting started

    • Quality and references

      Product masks, quality weights from similarity, coherence and inversion residuals, six reference methods, ionosphere and plate-motion corrections.

      Quality tutorials

    • Velocities and time series

      Trajectory fits, OLS, Whittle, MIDAS and GLS velocities with colored-noise sigmas, model diagnostics, Kalman and LOWESS denoising.

      Choosing a velocity method

    • GNSS validation

      Stations next to DISP through geepers, residuals against OPERA requirements, a GNSS tie that removes long-wavelength error.

      Comparing with GNSS

    • Downsample

      Quality-weighted grids, hexagons (planar and H3) and quadtrees, with cell uncertainties that account for spatial correlation.

      Downsampling

    • Export and serve

      Zarr v3, NetCDF, Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF, MintPy, Icechunk, GeoZarr for bowser, web map tiles, and region jobs for AWS Batch.

      Large frames and batch jobs
